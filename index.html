Want to open one for someone?

Launch a coin, type their handle, and the account is theirs from the first block. You get the coin. They get a balance that grows whether or not they ever look at it.

Withdrawal rules

Partial withdrawals are fine. The account stays open after a withdrawal and keeps taking deposits. Nothing expires, nothing is swept, nothing is burned on the handle's side. The only person who can move the balance is the person who can sign in as that handle.

02

Four steps. The split is written into the coin.

Set at launch, immutable after. Nobody can change it later, including us.

Launch

Name a handle

Launch on Pons, Robinhood Chain. Type any X handle: yours, a friend's, a stranger's, a company's. The handle does not need to know, agree, or exist on SavingsPad yet.

Deposit

Every trade pays in

Each buy and sell carries a 2% fee. 80% of it is deposited to the handle's account in USDG the moment the trade settles. 20% buys $SVNGS on the open market and burns it.

Compound

Interest posts daily

The account holds tokenized Treasury bills. Interest accrues every day at the reference rate and is added to principal, so tomorrow's interest is on today's interest too.

Withdraw

Sign in with X

The handle signs in, connects a wallet, and takes the balance. Principal, interest, everything. Free, any amount, any time. The account keeps working after they leave.

There is no protocol share. SavingsPad does not keep a cut of deposits, interest, or withdrawals. The 20% buys $SVNGS with fees and burns it in the same transaction, so every trade on every coin is a small, permanent bid.

Where the yield comes from

Treasury bills, not us

Deposits are converted into tokenized short-dated U.S. Treasury bills on Robinhood Chain. The reference rate tracks the 3-month bill and is published daily on every account page. When rates move, every account moves with them. We do not set the rate and cannot promise one.

What if nobody claims

It keeps compounding

An unclaimed account is not a problem to solve. It is a balance earning interest in someone's name. There is no expiry date and no sweep. Some of the largest accounts here belong to handles that have never signed in.

What if the coin dies

The account doesn't

Trading can stop tomorrow. The deposits already made stay in Treasury bills and keep posting interest. The coin and the account are two different things; only one of them needs volume.

Bonding curve and after

Same rules, both sides

Deposits are made from the first trade on the curve. When the coin graduates to Uniswap, the fee follows it and deposits continue. Nothing changes for the handle, nothing changes for the split.

Your wallet

Withdrawals land where you say

Connect a wallet on Robinhood Chain to set where your withdrawals go and to see your $SVNGS balance once it is live. Connecting shares your address and nothing else; SavingsPad never asks for a signature to read a balance.

Connect a wallet on Robinhood Chain to see your $SVNGS.
Wallets that work here

Robinhood Wallet supports Robinhood Chain out of the box. MetaMask, Rabby, Trust Wallet and OKX Wallet get the network added for you when you connect: chain ID 4663, ETH for gas, official RPC and explorer. Phone wallets connect by QR through WalletConnect.

Permanent bid

Twenty percent of every fee on every coin is a market buy of $SVNGS followed by a burn. Volume anywhere on SavingsPad is demand for the token, and the supply only goes down.

No treasury

SavingsPad holds nothing for itself. Deposits belong to handles, interest belongs to handles, and the buyback is burned rather than kept. There is no wallet to watch.

No emissions

One billion at launch, all of it. No staking rewards, no team unlock, no vesting schedule to wait out. The only supply event left is the burn.

Rate is not ours

Interest comes from the bills the accounts hold, not from token inflation. If the bill pays less, accounts earn less. Nobody is printing yield here.

06

Rollout.

Three stages. Nothing on this page is promised before its stage.

Now
  • Account lookup, statements and the calculator are live on this page.
  • $SVNGS launches on Pons. The split is written into the launch contract before the first coin.
  • Sign in with X is wired and audited before any balance can be released.
At launch
  • Launch a coin for any handle from the SavingsPad launch page.
  • First deposits land in T-bills the same day. First interest posts the next.
  • Public account pages with full deposit history and daily rate.
After
  • Monthly on-chain statements per handle.
  • Joint accounts: one coin, up to five handles, split written at launch.
  • Handles from other networks once their sign-in can be verified on-chain.
